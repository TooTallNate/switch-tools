New package: Zelda 64 (Ocarina of Time / Majora's Mask) objects and scenes. Object and scene tables located in `code` and named from the zeldaret decomps; skeletons (standard, LOD and flex) found structurally and drawn in one pass with a shared vertex cache, posed from an animation frame in the same object; scene / room headers, room shapes (normal, cullable, pre-rendered JPEG backgrounds) and whole-level meshes.
