Sonic Frontiers 3D models. `.pac` files open as archives, and their `.model` / `.terrain-model` files show textured in the viewer and the Library, with a Cyber Space variant where the materials have one. Materials and textures come from the same pac or its parent pacs, and streamed textures are rebuilt from `texture_streaming/*.ntsp`, so the pac's `.dds` files preview as whole images too.
