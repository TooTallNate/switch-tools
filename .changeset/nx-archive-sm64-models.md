Super Mario 64 models come out whole and animated: King Bob-omb's body, Bowser, doors, Chain Chomp's eyes, and hands and faces that were missing textures. Mario is a full model in five looks (cap, no cap, wing cap, Metal Mario, metal wing cap) with all 209 of his animations, and actors play their own animations in the viewer. Models are named after what they are (yoshi, king_bobomb, chain_chomp…) instead of `model_0x55`. Library search matches names before formats.
