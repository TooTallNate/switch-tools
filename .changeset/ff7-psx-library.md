Final Fantasy VII (PlayStation) models open in the 3D viewer and appear in the media library with thumbnails: every battle model (enemies, bosses, party members) and the field characters (Cloud, Barret, Tifa, Aerith, Red XIII, Yuffie, Cait Sith, Vincent, Cid). `.LZS` / `.BCX` files on PlayStation discs are recognised by parsing them, so other games' `.LZS` files are unaffected.
