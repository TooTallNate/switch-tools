New `@tootallnate/bflyt` package with a structural parser for NintendoWare layouts (`.bflyt`) and layout animations (`.bflan`). nx-archive shows a layout's canvas, textures, fonts, materials, groups and pane tree, and an animation's frames, groups and animated targets. `.flyt` / `.flan` / `.fcpx` LayoutEditor sources open as text, and BNTX banks with several textures (such as a layout's `__Combined.bntx`) get a texture picker.
