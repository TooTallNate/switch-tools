New package for Bandai Namco NUS3BANK sound banks (Pokkén Tournament DX, Smash 4). `parseNus3Bank` lists a bank's playable tones and skips random/sequence containers. `decodeIdsp` decodes Namco's interleaved multi-channel DSP-ADPCM streams to PCM, with loop points; the output is bit-exact against vgmstream.
